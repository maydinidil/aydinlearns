## Notes for the content generators

- **No overlap with 04.** The file kept to its brief: arc elasticity, cannibalisation, halo and
  promo uplift appear as ideas, not as 04's metric definitions. Readings link to the metric:
  ECON-PRICE-01 to `MET-PRICE-12` (arc elasticity), ECON-PRICE-03 to `MET-PRICE-05` and `-06`,
  ECON-PRICE-04 and -05 to `MET-PRICE-04`, and EXP-AB-13 to the conversion rate metric.
- **Interview evidence** is the author's judgement from about a dozen ads and Glassdoor reports,
  some for senior or data scientist roles. It is good enough to order the teaching and to pick the
  12 high-frequency concepts; it is not a statistic.
- **Common mistakes** are written to work as wrong options, as the prompt asked. One JSON entry
  (ECON-04's third) carries a citation inside the text; use the prose version.
- **Lehr's rule** is an approximation (80% power, two-sided 5%); items say so and accept only the
  rule's answer.
- **The worked examples** are the anchors for the typed items; the stems restate the inputs and
  the rounding.
